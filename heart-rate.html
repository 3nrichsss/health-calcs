How It Works

Maximum Heart Rate (MHR): Estimated using the Tanaka formula: 208 − 0.7 × age. This is more accurate than the older "220 − age" formula.

Target Zones (percentage of MHR):

  • Zone 1 (Very Light): 50–60% — warm-up, recovery
  • Zone 2 (Light): 60–70% — fat burning, endurance base
  • Zone 3 (Moderate): 70–80% — aerobic fitness
  • Zone 4 (Hard): 80–90% — anaerobic threshold, VO₂ max
  • Zone 5 (Maximum): 90–100% — peak performance, intervals

Karvonen Formula: Target HR = ((MHR − RHR) × % intensity) + RHR. This accounts for your resting heart rate for more personalized zones.

References: Tanaka H, et al. Age-predicted maximal heart rate revisited. J Am Coll Cardiol. 2001;38(1):153-156. Karvonen MJ, et al. The effects of training on heart rate. Ann Med Exp Biol Fenn. 1957;35:307-315.
