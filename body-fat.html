How It Works

This calculator uses the U.S. Navy body fat formula, which estimates body fat percentage from circumference measurements of the neck, waist, and (for women) hips, along with height.

Formula (men): 495 / (1.0324 − 0.19077 × log₁₀(waist − neck) + 0.15456 × log₁₀(height)) − 450

Formula (women): 495 / (1.29579 − 0.35004 × log₁₀(waist + hip − neck) + 0.22100 × log₁₀(height)) − 450

References: U.S. Navy. NAVPERS 15909C: Physical Readiness Program. Hodgdon JA, Beckett MB. Prediction of percent body fat for U.S. Navy men and women. NOSC Technical Report TR 84-29, 1984.
