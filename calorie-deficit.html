How It Works

This calculator uses the Mifflin-St Jeor equation to estimate your Basal Metabolic Rate (BMR), then applies an activity factor to determine your Total Daily Energy Expenditure (TDEE). Your calorie target is TDEE minus your chosen deficit.

One kg of body fat ≈ 7,700 kcal. Projected weight loss = (deficit × days) ÷ 7,700.

References: Mifflin MD, et al. Am J Clin Nutr. 1990;51(2):241-247. Wishnofsky M. Am J Clin Nutr. 1958;6(5):555-558.
